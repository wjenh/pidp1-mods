This light pen test starts at address 0.
It uses the sense switches to pick a test.
When started, it just loops looking for a sense switch to be set.
Switches 1-4 select one of 4 different tests.

Switch 5 is not used.
Switch 6 has a special meaning, stop test. When set, the program returns to address 0.

Switch 1 test:
    Two small vertical lines are displayed. Moving the light pen over either will show an expanded
    view of the pixels seen by the light pen, move the pen up and down over the lines to see this.
    This test is stopped by turning the switch off.

Switch 2 test:
    A box with a bright horizontal line that sweeps from top to bottom is displayed.
    Move the light pen around inside it.
    On success, OK is displayed.
    This test is stopped by turning the switch off.

Switch 3 test:
    A small box is displayed.
    Moving the light pen around in the box shows an expanded view of the pixels seen.
    This test is stopped by turning the switch off.

Switch 4 test:
    The screen is filled with dots that are continually redrawn from botton to top.
    A light pen hit on a dot stops the drawing at that point.
    The intent of this test seems to have been to detect hits over the entire range of x, y coordinates.
    The test resumes when the pen is lifted.
    This test must be stopped via switch 6.
