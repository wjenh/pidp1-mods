This exercises the Type 30 display checking bounds, and coordinates.
The edges are difficult to see because everything is one pixel!
It is cohtrolled by the sense switches.

All dowwn - draws a full-range diagonal from lower left to upper right
If multiple are down, the lowest number takes precedence.

1 up - draw dots spaced apart on the left and right edges
2 up - draW dotted diagonals from corner to corner
3 up - draW closely spaced dotted horizontal and vertical lines from the edge midpoints, should intersect in the center
4 up - draW one dot in the center, dotted corner markers, dotted edge markers centered at side midpoints
5 up - draw a scanning rectangular area depending upon test swith settings.9-11 for the bottom part of the screen,
       12-14 for the center, 15-17 for the top, each switch controlling one of left, middle, or right.
       Each area is drawn in turn from left to right.
       The switches can be combined.
6 up - draw a scanning rectangular area depending upon test swith settings.9-11 for the bottom part of the screen,
       12-14 for the center, 15-17 for the top, each switch controlling one of left, middle, or right.
       Each area is drawn in turn from top downwards.
       The switches can be combined.
